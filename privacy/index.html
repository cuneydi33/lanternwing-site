Lanternwing: Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

Lanternwing collects no personal data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to any server of ours.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. They are included in your own device backups (for example iCloud Backup) the way any app's files are; we never see them.

Purchases

The in-app purchase (Full Flight Licence) is handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID; the game only learns from Apple whether the purchase is yours.

Children

Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either.
