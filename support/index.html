Lanternwing: Support

Write to viralexee@gmail.com and tell us your device and what happened. We answer within two days.

I bought Full Flight but it is locked

Open Settings → Restore (it is also on the Full Flight screen). Use the Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) that made the purchase. The game also checks your purchases with the store each time it starts.

My progress on a new phone

Progress is saved on the device. On iPhone, move it by restoring your old phone's backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) to the new one. On Android, progress stays on the device it was played on. Full Flight comes back with Restore on either.

Refunds

Refunds go through the store you bought from: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play at Google Play's refund page.

See also the privacy policy.
