Lanternwing: Support

Write to viralexee@gmail.com and tell us your device and what happened. We answer within two days.

I bought Full Flight but it is locked

Open Settings → Restore (it is also on the Full Flight screen). Sign in with the Apple ID that made the purchase. The game also checks your purchases with Apple each time it starts.

My progress on a new phone

Progress is saved on the device. Move it by restoring your old phone's backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) to the new one. Full Flight comes back with Restore.

Refunds

Refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
